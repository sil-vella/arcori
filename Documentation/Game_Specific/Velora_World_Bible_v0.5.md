## The Origin of Velora

No one knows when Velora first came into existence.

The oldest Chronicles do not describe its birth. They speak only of an age before memory, when the world had no sky, no lands and no names.

It is said that Velora was never created in the way ordinary worlds are. It did not emerge from fire. It was not shaped from stone. Nor was it born among the stars.

Instead, Velora awakened within the quiet space where memories outlive moments, where imagination survives long after hands have let go.

Some believe every age leaves behind an echo—an imprint too meaningful to disappear. Most fade into silence. A few become something greater.

Velora is said to be one of those echoes.

Its rivers carry forgotten dreams. Its mountains stand upon ancient joys. Its forests whisper stories no one remembers telling.

Every Arcori bears a fragment of that forgotten age, though none can truly explain why.

The Museum preserves what is known.  
The Chronicle remembers what cannot be proven.

In play: **Velora** is the world **Avari** enter to discover what still circulates (Arcori most of all). Mastery is progress upon those living echoes — not ownership. When a generation closes and a mint is earned, that Arcori leaves circulation into an Avari’s **Trove**; the Museum keeps the world’s factual record of what closed.

Somewhere beyond every region yet discovered lies the unanswered question every generation inherits:

Who first dreamed of Velora?

## The Avari

Avari are those who walk beside the Arcori. Though they do not command their destinies, they shape their journeys through skill, perseverance, and the preservation of Legacy. Every Chronicle remembers the Arcori, but it also remembers the Avari who carried them through the ages.

Among the Avari, the Chronicles mark those who rise further:

| Name | Kind | How the Chronicle speaks of them |
|------|------|----------------------------------|
| **Avari** | Identity | All who walk beside the Arcori |
| **Master** | Competitive title | Proven through skill and standing |
| **Legacy Owner** | Preservation achievement | Those who keep a closed Arcori from vanishing into silence |
| **Generation Creator** | Historical title | Named when a generation’s origin is attributed to an Avari’s hand |

Identity first; titles after. An Avari remains Avari even when the Chronicle also names them Master, Legacy Owner, or Generation Creator.

## The Great Separation (Future Revelation Draft)

There are those who believe the First Hands never truly left.

The oldest Chronicles tell of a time when the boundary between Velora and the Realm Beyond was so thin that every laugh, every hope and every act of wonder echoed freely between them.

It was remembered only as The Bright Time.

Then the echoes began to fade.

Something changed beyond the veil.

The Realm Beyond grew heavier.

Its people became burdened by struggles the people of Velora could scarcely imagine. Their days became shorter, their laughter quieter, and the moments that once gave Velora life became increasingly rare.

The First Hands did not abandon Velora. They simply found themselves unable to return.

Yet every memory they carried, every joyful moment they refused to lose, gathered beyond the veil until it became something new.

From those echoes, Velora awakened—not as a replacement for what had been lost, but as a sanctuary where those moments could live on.

Only one question survives every generation:

If the First Hands still exist... do they remember us?

## Future Chronicle Roadmap

**Phase 1:** References to the First Hands and mysterious carvings of enormous hands surrounding perfect circles.

**Phase 2:** Discovery of forgotten civilizations. Scholars question why every Arcori shares the same perfect circular form. The Realm Beyond is first mentioned.

**Phase 3:** Different cultures develop competing beliefs about the First Hands. Some believe they created Velora; others believe they merely held it long enough for it to remember itself.

**Phase 4:** The community gradually infers that Arcori are echoes of tangible objects once held by the First Hands. The Chronicle never explicitly confirms this. The mystery remains part of Velora's mythology.
